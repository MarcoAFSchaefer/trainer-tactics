# Changelog

## 0.3.0

- The Auto battle and Advisor switches now belong to the save that is open, kept in the game's per-save store for mods; they used to be the same for every save and every open copy of the game. A save with nothing of its own yet starts from what there was.

## 0.2.0

- The Tactics panel carries the Tactics Badge on its tab in Trainer Desk (0.8.0 or newer; older versions just show the title).
- The mod is now part of the **Trainer Badges**: a badge, a banner and the family case in the README.

## 0.1.0

- First version: the battle brain, the advisor (battle screen box and Trainer Desk panel) and auto battle.
- API for other mods; Trainer Pilot 0.30.0 uses it for its battles.
- English, Spanish, Portuguese and Japanese.
