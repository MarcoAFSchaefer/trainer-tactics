# Differences from the unmodded game

Trainer Tactics works out the best play of a battle turn. It can show it or play it; it does not change how battles work.

## Game data and rules

Nothing. No species, moves, items, trainers or battle rules are changed. Damage numbers come from the game's own damage function, so they are what the game would do.

## What the unmodded game does not show you

- The damage each of your moves would do, as a range in % of the foe's HP.
- With **Foe moves** set to KNOWN, the opponent's real moves and what they would do to you. Set it to GUESSED to assume only one plain attack of each of the foe's types.

## What it does that a player would otherwise do

**Auto battle** (off by default) chooses moves and switches through the game's own battle menus. It never uses items, never runs from wild Pokémon, and leaves move learning, nicknames and evolution to you.

## Screen and input

A small advice box on the battle screen (can be turned off) and a **Tactics** panel in Trainer Desk. With Auto battle on, the mod sends button presses during battles; nothing else about input changes.

## Saves

The save file is not read or written by the mod. Settings are kept in the mod's own cache.

## Turning it off

Disable the mod and battles are as before. Nothing is left in the save.
