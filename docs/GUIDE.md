# Trainer Tactics: player guide

## The advice

Start any battle. While the menu or your moves are showing, a small box appears on the left of the battle screen:

- the best play ("Use Surf 62-74%" or "Switch to Venusaur");
- two more of your attacks with their damage;
- a warning when the foe can knock your Pokémon out this turn, or when HP is low.

Damage is a range in % of the target's current HP, from the weakest to the strongest roll, without a critical hit. "62-74%" means two hits will do it; "105-124%" is a certain knock-out if it lands.

With Trainer Desk, the **Tactics** panel shows more: all four moves with accuracy and notes, each move of the foe against you, and your team with what each one would give and take.

## Auto battle

Turn on **Auto battle** (panel switch, mod option, or a key bound with Trainer Controls). From then on Tactics answers the battle menus. It stops for questions that are yours: a new move to learn, a nickname. Turn it off the same way at any time.

It does not use items and does not run from wild Pokémon. If its team is losing it will keep fighting, so keep an eye on tough battles.

## Reading the notes

| Note | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| KO / may KO | Even the weakest roll knocks out / only a strong roll does. |
| hits first | It knocks out before the foe moves. |
| no effect | Type or ability immunity. |
| two turns / recharges | Charging and recharging moves count for less. |
| may miss | Accuracy under 100%. |
| status / set-up / heals | Not an attack; chosen only when it pays. |
| risky (team) | It may not survive coming in. |

## Foe moves

By default Tactics reads the foe's real moves, as the game's own trainers read yours. If you prefer not to know, set **Foe moves** to GUESSED: it then assumes one plain attack of each of the foe's types.
